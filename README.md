# UNIVA – AI-Powered Career Guidance & Learning Platform 🚀

**UNIVA** is an AI-powered career guidance and learning platform designed to help college students and fresh graduates discover suitable career paths, understand their skill gaps, create personalized learning roadmaps, and explore relevant opportunities.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://jyoshnijuthuka.github.io/UNIVA/

---

## 🌟 About UNIVA

Choosing the right career can be difficult for students because they often don't know:

- Which career path suits their interests and strengths
- What skills are required for a particular career
- Which skills they are currently missing
- What they should learn next
- Which courses, projects, internships, or opportunities are relevant to them

**UNIVA** aims to bring these different aspects together in one platform using AI-powered guidance.

### Our Vision

> **Your Future. Your Skills. Your Path.**

UNIVA helps learners move from **career confusion → skill discovery → personalized learning → career opportunities**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 Career Discovery
Helps students explore career paths based on their interests, strengths, and goals.

### 📊 Skill Gap Analysis
Identifies the skills required for a chosen career and helps learners understand areas where they need improvement.

### 🗺️ Personalized Learning Roadmaps
Provides a structured learning path to help students develop the skills needed for their target career.

### 📚 Learning Recommendations
Helps learners discover relevant learning resources and courses based on their career goals and skill gaps.

### 💼 Opportunity Discovery
Connects learners with relevant opportunities such as:

- Internships
- Projects
- Courses
- Jobs
- Skill-building opportunities

### 🤖 AI-Powered Guidance
Uses AI to provide personalized recommendations and guidance based on the learner's interests, skills, and career goals.
